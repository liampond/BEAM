For the "How many notes are in the __ hand in this measure" questions, a bar was chosen at random, except when the passage contained:
- A trill, since the MusicXML encoding writes them out and the other formats use a trill symbol
- Rendering of the LH in the upper staff or vice versa, since we don't expect the LLM to infer which hand should play what.
