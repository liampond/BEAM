### Multi-Measure Analysis
"How many times does the pitch C5 appear in measures 1-4?"
"In which measure (1, 2, or 3) does the highest pitch occur?"
"What is the total number of notes in both hands combined across measures 1-3?"
"What is the melodic interval between the first note in measure 1 and the last note in measure 3?"
### Cross-Voice/Hand Relationships
"How many times do both hands play simultaneously on beat 1 of this measure?"
"What is the largest harmonic interval (in semitones) between the right and left hands at any simultaneous moment in this measure?"
"On which beat does the left hand cross above the right hand's pitch?" (if applicable)
"How many times do both hands play the same pitch class simultaneously?"
### Precise Timing & Rhythm
"What is the exact beat position (e.g., 1.75) where the second sixteenth note begins in measure 2?"
"What is the total duration of all rests (in beats) in measures 1-3?"
"How many notes in measure 2 begin on off-beats (not on 1, 2, 3, or 4)?"
"What fraction of measure 1 is occupied by sounding notes in the right hand (excluding rests)?"
### Sequential Patterns
"How many times does the Alberti bass pattern repeat in measures 1-4?"
"Does the left-hand pattern in measure 2 match the pattern in measure 1?" (yes/no)
"How many times does the melodic interval of a major third appear in the right hand across measures 1-3?"
### Range & Extremes
"What is the pitch range (in semitones) of the right hand in measure 1?"
"What is the largest melodic interval (in semitones) in the right hand in this measure?"
"Which hand covers a wider pitch range in measure 1?" (right/left/equal)
### Voice Leading
"How many ascending melodic intervals occur in the right hand in measure 1?"
"What is the predominant direction of melodic motion in the right hand?" (ascending/descending/static)
"How many melodic leaps (intervals larger than a perfect fourth) occur in the right hand?"
### Comparative Questions
"Which measure (1, 2, or 3) has the most notes in the right hand?"
"Do measures 1 and 3 have the same number of notes in the left hand?" (yes/no)
### Calculation-Based
"What is the average pitch (as MIDI note number) of all notes in the right hand in measure 1?"
"What is the ratio of quarter notes to eighth notes in the left hand in measure 1?"
